Rows come from the "docs/FEATURE_MAP.md entries" sections of [PROTOTYPE-PLAN.md](PROTOTYPE-PLAN.md),
[PUZZLES-PLAN.md](PUZZLES-PLAN.md), [STORIES-PLAN.md](STORIES-PLAN.md) and [META-PLAN.md](META-PLAN.md), cut to what

   *  may leave with it; absent = `'flush'` (§8.1 "When a cue's DOM target is found"). */

   *  begins (a `shake`, a `flourish`) or where the status line's motion begins (`ready`, `offline`, `rate-limited`).

   *  `ready`, `renewed`, `reset`) + `puzzle:closed`: the keys that end `busy` or the frame. */

   *  and a cue whose every motion was skipped (an element-site motion at `edge`); the `centre` and `edge` landings
   *  never count. */

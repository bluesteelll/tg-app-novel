# Promotion research 13: TikTok and YouTube in Russia through VPN (short video for a Russian-first romance VN)
Date 2026-10-31. Sources read: 41 (opened) + 6 search-snippet-only items, marked as such. Freshness notes: TikTok ToS
(ROW) "Last updated 1 December 2025"; TikTok Ads help pages "Last updated June 2025 / November 2025"; YouTube Help
"Sharing links" page undated (©2026); the newest audience figures found are Mediascope Dec 2025 / Jan 2026 (via press)
and H1 2026 non-Mediascope studies (via Kommersant, Jul-Sep 2026). No Mediascope TikTok figure for any 2026 month was
found. The Telegram-side facts reused (startapp) come from `research_M2.md` and were not re-fetched.

Scope: the owner's ruling (2026-10-31) is that Russian TikTok and YouTube are alive because many people post and watch
through a VPN. 04 and PROMOTION.md §4.4-4.5 treated TikTok as "n/a for RU creators" and YouTube as "Avoid (RU paid)".
This report sizes and shapes that channel. It does not re-argue the ruling.

## TL;DR (decision-relevant)

- **Both platforms still reach very large Russian audiences, by the measurer's own panel.** TikTok had 65.9 M monthly
  and 27.3 M daily users in Dec 2025 (Mediascope, 12+). That is down from a 69.8 M peak in Jan 2025 (CLAIMED: blog
  citing Mediascope) [9]. Among 12-17-year-olds TikTok was the No. 1 network: 61 % daily reach in Jan-Jul 2024 (press
  citing Mediascope) [11]. Teens spent 73 min/day on it, and 18-24s spent 48 min/day (Dec 2025, CLAIMED) [9]. YouTube
  had 65.9 M monthly and ~22 M daily users in Jan 2026 (Mediascope via Moscow Times) [15]. Its DNS-level block on
  2026-02-10/12 [15][16] did not shrink it: daily reach rose 4 % to 22.4 M in Jan-May 2026 (Rodnaya Rech via
  Kommersant) [17], and time per user rose 24 % to 99 min/day in H1 2026 (Future Lab) [18]. All CLAIMED (press
  citing studies).
- **What "TikTok restricted" means in 2026.** TikTok imposed the restriction itself in early March 2022; RKN never
  blocked it [1][3]. From a Russian IP the app shows a pre-March-2022 archive, with no uploads, no lives and no
  monetization. From a **foreign IP the official app works normally**: new videos, the trends feed and uploads (T-Zh
  author test, 2026-05-05; Postium 2026-03-11) [2][3]. Older workarounds are reported dead in 2026: phone-region
  change and a foreign eSIM without a foreign IP [2]. Mods remain a fallback with malware and account risks [4].
  All CLAIMED (practitioner tests). No platform statement from 2026 was found.
- **The VPN audience is large and young.** In a phone survey of 1,600 people (Apr 15-22, 2026), 40 % of Russians said
  they actively use a VPN: 61 % of 18-29s, 62 % in Moscow (Russian Field via SecurityLab) [20]. That is a MEASURED
  survey with a stated method; the 12-17 group was not covered. The VPN-using viewers who see fresh TikTok content are
  the same people who can still open Telegram under the 2026 restrictions (ANALYSIS).
- **Brands are moving money back to TikTok in Russia.** Win2Win (Dec 2025, 1,800+ bloggers) found that the number of
  bloggers offering TikTok integrations grew 6x in a year, at an average of 1.26 ₽ per view. Shorts cost 1.30 ₽ per
  view and VK Clips 3 ₽ [30]. DIDENOK TEAM (Jul 2025-Jul 2026, 650 TikTok authors) puts TikTok at **1.57-2.61 ₽ per
  view**: beauty/fashion 2.61 ₽, gaming 1.80 ₽, celebrities 4.50 ₽ [29]. All CLAIMED (agency price-list studies).
- **Linking out is constrained on both platforms.** YouTube: URLs in **Shorts descriptions and comments are not
  clickable**. Channel profile links (up to 14), long-form descriptions and "related video" links on Shorts are
  clickable (DOCUMENTED) [23]. TikTok: a bio website link needs, per guides, 1,000+ followers or a business account
  (CLAIMED) [24]. App-store links are business-only (TechCrunch 2023) [24]. No source confirms or rules out a `t.me`
  link in a TikTok bio (gap). Separately, `t.me` itself failed at the global DNS level in Jul 2026 (CLAIMED, one
  source) [25].
- **Paid self-serve ads cannot target Russia.** Google Ads has shown no ads to users in Russia since early March 2022,
  and Russian cards cannot pay [28]. TikTok businesses "cannot target Russia" (eLama 2022) [28]. TikTok's 2025 help
  page still lists Russia among countries that can *create* ad accounts, but the targeting list was not readable
  [26][27]. Paid reach on these platforms in 2026 means creator integrations, with ERID marking (area 06; the owner
  accepts the ad-law risk).
- **Evidence gaps that matter.** (a) No source measures how many Russian TikTok users actually see *new* content.
  (b) No 2026 TikTok Mediascope figure was found. (c) There is no case of a Russian romance VN, otome game or
  Romance Club fan account on TikTok/Shorts with numbers. (d) TikTok's ToS forbids "bypass[ing] any measures we may
  use to … restrict access" (DOCUMENTED) [8]. Region spoofing is therefore a ToS violation; how often it is enforced
  is unknown.

### 2. How Russian creators post in 2026

- **What is restricted, and by whom.** TikTok, not RKN, introduced the restriction in early March 2022 [1][3]. With a
  Russian IP: "new videos are officially not uploaded from Russian accounts", no lives, "global recommendations are
  unavailable", and the feed shows only pre-March-2022 videos. Links to new videos do not open; TikTok Lite is the same
  (Unisender 2026-01-20) [1]. "The Russian version of the app has effectively turned into an archive of old videos"
  (Postium 2026-03-11) [3]. RKN keeps TikTok in its social-network register, and it is not banned; there was "no
  direct threat of a full block" as of Mar 2026 [3]. All CLAIMED (explainers).
- **No 2026 platform statement found.** V2 row 24 cited "TikTok denied restrictions were lifted (kod.ru 2026-07-22,
  search snippet)". The opened page (kod.ru/6162) is dated **2022-05-03** ("В сервисе TikTok нет изменений", "There
  are no changes to the TikTok service") [7]. The "22 июля 2026" was the site header date. A May 2024 one-day partial
  unlock was reverted the same day, and content posted in that window disappeared (ppc.world 2024-05-07) [6]. The last
  TikTok statement found is therefore from 2022.
- **Methods that work in 2026, per practitioner tests (CLAIMED).**
  - **Foreign IP (VPN) in the official app** "opened almost all familiar functions": no region change, new account or
    reinstall needed. Some readers report glitches; the fix offered is to switch server country and clear the cache
    (T-Zh 2026-05-05, author's own test) [2]. Postium: "via a foreign region the app starts working as usual": new
    videos, trends, recommendations [3].
  - **Web upload** with a foreign IP: one video up to 60 min, a basic editor, edit elsewhere (CapCut) [2]. Uploads are
    slow over a VPN [5].
  - **Mods** (Android; iOS via AltServer, re-signed weekly): a fallback. They spoof the country sent to TikTok's
    servers and fail on Russian, Indian, Nepali, Azerbaijani and Afghan IPs. Reported harms: sudden "too many login
    attempts" lockouts, lost subscriptions and likes, and trojanised builds that steal credentials (Lenta 2026-03-30,
    experts quoted) [4][2].
  - **Dead in 2026:** phone-region change alone, and a foreign eSIM without a foreign IP [2]. Contrast: Mail.ru Hi-Tech
    (2025-08-18) still recommended eSIM and region change [5]. Methods change within months.
- **Who sees a new Russian-language video.** Viewers with a foreign IP (VPN users in Russia), Russian speakers in CIS
  countries and the diaspora; Russian IPs without a workaround see nothing new (ppc.world 2024, creators quoted;
  Postium 2026) [6][3]. No measurement exists of the reachable share (gap). Glitches reported: a video disappearing
  seconds after upload, distorted analytics, dropped lives [3].
- **Account-ban risk.** TikTok ToS (ROW, 2025-12-01), §5: users may not "bypass any measures we may use to prevent or
  restrict access to the Services"; §4: "must provide accurate and up-to-date information"; TikTok may "disable your
  user account at any time" for violations. VPNs are not named. Minimum age 13 (§11(e)). Commercial use needs
  "express written consent" (§5) (DOCUMENTED [8]; INTERPRETATION: a business account is the consented commercial
  route). Explainers say spoofing "may be treated as suspicious activity and the profile blocked" [1][3] (CLAIMED).
  No ban statistics were found.
- **Monetization.** TikTok pays Russian creators nothing (creator fund and live gifts off) [1][3]. On YouTube, Russian
  monetization has been unavailable since 2022 (CLAIMED, partnerkin snippet), and Google stopped serving ads to users
  in Russia in Mar 2022 [28]. Creators live on integrations, which keeps integration supply high.
- **YouTube posting from Russia.** After the Feb 2026 DNS block the site is "unavailable without a VPN" [15], so
  uploads need one too. The block works through DNS; experts call it "easily bypassed by changing DNS or tunnelling",
  and YouTube's infrastructure in Russia still runs [15]. No source describes upload failures or channel penalties for
  Russian uploaders (gap). Shorts upload rules are global; no Russia-specific rule was found.

### 3. Promotion practice

- **Russian romance/otome cases with numbers.** None was found for TikTok or Shorts (gap). Romance Club's marketing
  writeup (Unisender, upd. 2026-04-21) cites VK 1.4 M and Telegram 700 K+. It describes short social video ads with
  "absurd, sometimes explicit dialogue" that send people to the app for the scene's resolution, plus UGC guides and
  memes. It does not name TikTok or YouTube [39] (CLAIMED). Attempts to read TikTok hashtag pages failed (JS-only).
- **BookTok-RU.** Eksmo (2024-05-29) cites BookTok as "one of the main drivers of book growth". Formats that work, per
  Eksmo: 12-second clips, trailer cuts, excerpt readings, cosplay, aesthetic covers. Its example "Mara and Morok" (Lia
  Arden) is a long-running BookTok hit with fan cosplay and readings [33]. That is a publisher's marketing piece
  (CLAIMED); no Russian sales figures were found.
- **The closest VN case (global, dated).** "I Just Want to be Single!!", a dating-sim VN by Tsunderestudio, posted a
  TikTok showing just its main menu. It reached 571 K views over several days and brought 2,900+ Steam wishlists
  (~3 K → 6 K in a month). Lessons quoted: hook in the first second, a homemade look (screen capture plus TTS) beat
  polish, a clear on-screen CTA for ~3 s, and character-introduction videos. Bio UTM links tracked only ~10 % of
  traffic (howtomarketagame 2022-02-07) [34]. CLAIMED (single case, 2022, Steam not Telegram).
- **Formats for romance stories (from the sources above).** Character introductions and edits set to trending audio;
  a cliffhanger scene cut before the resolution (Romance Club's ad pattern [39]); "can YOU …" challenge hooks and
  niche-fan references [34]; aesthetic or cosplay content [33]. "Choose your love interest" and POV formats got no
  sourced data (gap). These are formats, not measured conversion.
- **Linking from video to a Telegram bot.**
  - **YouTube (DOCUMENTED [23]).** Clickable: channel profile links (up to 14), URLs in long-form descriptions and
    comments, related-video links on Shorts, end screens. Not clickable: "URLs placed in YouTube Shorts comments and
    Shorts descriptions". Clickable external links in long-form content need "advanced features" enabled on the
    channel. Implication: a Short → its related long video or the channel page → the clickable link. A pinned comment
    on a Short carries no clickable link.
  - **TikTok.** Per third-party guides, the bio website field requires 1,000+ followers or a business account; one
    link only, app-only, sometimes unavailable by region (CLAIMED) [24]. Since 2023-03, app-store links in bios work
    only for business accounts (TechCrunch 2023-03-08, TikTok quoted) [24]. Whether TikTok accepts or blocks `t.me` in
    the bio field: no source (gap). One link-in-bio vendor says the block list is unpublished (search snippet).
  - **The `t.me` domain itself.** On ~2026-07-16 the .me registry's DNS stopped resolving `t.me` worldwide; Telegram
    moved links to `telegram.me`; the app kept working (tech.az 2026-07-16) [25]. CLAIMED, one source. An own-domain
    landing page would not depend on a single Telegram domain (ANALYSIS).
- **VK Clips as the domestic mirror.** VK Clips had 3.4 B average daily views in Q1 2026 (company-reported) [37] and
  3.1 B in Q4 2025 [38]. Clips passed TikTok in monthly reach in Jun 2024 (66.6 M vs 64.3 M, Mediascope) [14]. By
  VK's own creator study (2024-10-09), 73 % of Clips creators are women and 20 % are 18-24 (creators, not viewers)
  [35]. VK's audience is 54-64 % female by "different methods", source not named (Sostav blog 2026-07-28) [36]. All
  CLAIMED. VK needs no VPN, so the same vertical clip can be posted to VK Clips, TikTok and Shorts.
- **Influencer prices, Russian creators (CLAIMED, agency price-list studies).**
  - DIDENOK TEAM (Jul 2025-Jul 2026; TikTok n = 650, YouTube 700, VK 500, Telegram 550). TikTok 1.57-2.61 ₽ per view:
    beauty/fashion 2.61, humour 2.60, gaming 1.80, kids 1.57, celebrities 4.50. YouTube: beauty/fashion 3.70 ₽,
    entertainment 1.53 ₽. VK Clips 2.80-12 ₽. No Shorts figure (Sostav 2026-08-26) [29][32].
  - Win2Win (2025, 1,800+ bloggers): TikTok 1.26 ₽ (+18.9 %), YouTube Shorts 1.30 ₽ (+78.1 %), VK Clips 3.00 ₽,
    Telegram 4.42 ₽ (Sostav 2025-12-15) [30].
  - No romance-, female-teen- or VN-niche price was found. At 1.3-2.6 ₽ per view, 100 K views cost ~130-260 K ₽
    (ANALYSIS on CLAIMED inputs).

### 4. Paid options

- **Google Ads / YouTube ads.** Since early March 2022: "ads launched in Google are not shown to users in our
  country", and Russian cards and accounts cannot pay even for foreign targeting (eLama 2022-04-01) [28]. Google
  paused new Russian AdSense/AdMob accounts on 2022-03-10 (Google support, search snippet). No later change was found
  (DOCUMENTED-by-press, 2022; no 2026 re-check possible without an account).
- **TikTok Ads.** In 2022 "business cannot target Russia; ad cabinets remain active for foreign users" [28]. Unisender
  2026: "TikTok's official ad cabinet does not work in Russia" [1]. Contradiction to note: TikTok's help page (last
  updated Nov 2025) lists **Russia** among countries for self-serve ad-account *creation* [26]. The page of deliverable
  markets (Jun 2025) did not render its country list [27]. So whether a Russian-registered account can deliver ads to
  Russia is unverified (gap). The practitioner consensus is no (CLAIMED).
- **The working paid route** is buying creator integrations: native mentions, placement, promo codes [1]. Any paid
  placement needs ERID marking and a creator who is on RKN's list above 10 K followers (Unisender; area 06). The owner
  accepts the ad-law risk.

## Applicability to tg-app-novel (map onto §2 planned features; product feature asks, listed not designed)

- **Fit (ANALYSIS).** For women aged 14-25 in Russia, TikTok is the measurer's No. 1 time sink among teens. YouTube
  retains a VPN-using core that skews 18-34. The channel is zero-cash but labour-heavy, and the evidence for *our*
  conversion is weak: no romance-VN case with numbers, and no measured share of viewers who see new content. There is
  one structural advantage: a viewer who sees a new Russian TikTok in Russia is, by construction, on a VPN or abroad.
  That viewer can also open the restricted Telegram. VK Clips gives the no-VPN part of the same audience for the same
  clip.
- **Assets the product must supply** (free-chapter material only; minors-safe; never the 18+ track or any paid
  scene):
  1. Vertical 9:16 screen captures of chapter-1 scenes, 7-30 s, hooked in the first second. One cut ends on the
     paywall cliffhanger before its resolution (the Romance Club ad pattern [39]).
  2. Character introduction cards and stills (one per love interest), usable for edits set to trending audio [34].
  3. A clean capture mode (no debug UI, no Telegram chrome), plus subtitles in Russian, with English as a second track.
  4. Asset-licence clarity for fans and creators: which art and music they may reuse in edits.
- **Product asks (listed, not designed).**
  1. A **per-creator / per-platform campaign code** in `startapp` (for example a short code namespace distinct from
     M10 invite codes). `start_param` arrives in signed initData and is attributed on `POST /api/auth` (M11;
     research_M2 lines 81-93). The server needs a campaign table and reporting of newcomers and first-chapter
     completion per code.
  2. A **link-in-bio landing page on an own domain**: one URL per platform/creator, a 302 or button to
     `t.me/<bot>?startapp=<code>`, and a `telegram.me` fallback. It covers a single-link bio, the
     Shorts-to-channel-link hop and the Jul 2026 `t.me` DNS failure [25]. It must not carry paid content (principle 0)
     and must not use a client-trusted unlock (principle 1).
  3. **A short, speakable entry path** for clips where links are not clickable (Shorts descriptions [23]): a bot
     username that can be said aloud or typed, plus a code entry (or a deep-link payload) so attribution survives.
  4. **Cohort reporting** that separates platforms (TikTok / Shorts / VK Clips / per creator) for the M11 attribution
     and the PROMOTION.md §5 funnel.
  5. **A minors-safe content flag on clips**: an export check that no 18+ or paid asset enters the marketing-capture
     set, mirroring the paid-content gate.

## Pitfalls / what goes wrong (sourced)

- **Posting with a Russian IP silently fails.** Videos disappear seconds after upload, analytics are distorted and
  lives drop [3]. During the May 2024 window, content posted vanished after access was reverted [6].
- **Mods steal accounts.** Trojanised builds, login lockouts and lost likes/subscriptions [4]. Do not run a brand
  account on a mod.
- **Workarounds rot within months.** The eSIM and region-change methods recommended in Aug 2025 [5] were reported
  dead by May 2026 [2].
- **Region spoofing breaches TikTok ToS §5** ("bypass any measures … restrict access") [8]. The account is the asset at
  risk.
- **Shorts links are dead text** [23]. A "link in pinned comment" plan on a Short gives no click.
- **A single bio link with a `t.me` target is a single point of failure** (the `t.me` DNS failure, Jul 2026 [25]).
- **Bio UTM tracking undercounts.** ~10 % of traffic was tracked in the 2022 VN case [34]. Expect start_param codes to
  miss viewers who search the bot by name.
- **Stale "facts" propagate.** V2's "TikTok denial 2026-07-22" was a 2022 article [7]. Site header dates on 3DNews and
  kod.ru are often mistaken for article dates (observed on [10][14][19]).
- **Russian anti-VPN policy can shrink the reachable audience.** Platform-side VPN restrictions and traffic
  surcharges were proposed in 2026 (snippets, Key claim 11).

## Owner value questions this area raises (each with a default reading)

1. **Who posts, from where?** The operator's own TikTok account posting from Russia breaches ToS §5 by bypassing the
   restriction. Posting from abroad, or via a non-Russian team member, does not (INTERPRETATION). Default: the brand
   account is run from outside Russia, or creators post for us. Decided together with Q21 (jurisdiction).
2. **Own account vs creators first?** Default: a small own presence (TikTok + Shorts + VK Clips cross-post of the
   same clips) from soft launch, plus 2-3 test integrations with per-creator codes before scaling.
3. **Creators under 18 / audiences 14-17.** Default: adult creators only. Free-chapter, minors-safe clips only. Never
   the 18+ track on these platforms.
4. **Fan edits of our art and music.** Default: allow non-commercial fan edits of free-chapter art, with a published
   short licence note. The music licence decides whether our tracks can be used (open, Q2).
5. **English TikTok.** Default: the same clips with English subtitles via the same account, for CIS/diaspora/EN reach.
   A separate EN account comes later.

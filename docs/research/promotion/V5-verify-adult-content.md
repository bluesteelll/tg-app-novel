## Most decision-relevant corrections

1. **An age gate or a paid purchase is still "distribution" (Plenum 37 p. 22).** Plenum 37 p. 22 counts giving access to
   specific persons as distribution, even one person by private message. Prior free availability does not matter. So
   selling a pornographic chapter for Stars to one verified adult is still distribution under art. 242, and part 3 "б"
   (2-6 years) applies because the internet is used.
2. **Text alone has been convicted.** In 2026-05, fan-fiction books without pictures led to a conviction under 242 part 3
   "б", after a psycholinguistic examination: 1.5 years of forced labour. The case was triggered by a minor's parent. A
   text-first VN is not a safe lane for explicit description.
3. **An expert can assign a minor age to drawn characters.** Bryansk: an expert found "male persons under 14" in
   undated hentai drawings, and the court convicted under 242.1 part 1 (2019, retrial 2020-06). Youthful anime-style
   characters in sexual scenes therefore carry 242.1 exposure (2-8 years, 3-10 online) whatever age the script states.
   The note 2 artistic-value exemption covers only depictions of genitals, never sexual acts.
4. **The Stars forfeiture clause is Bot Developer Terms §6.2.4, not Stars ToS §5.** Stars ToS §5 is about the user's
   personal balance. §6.2.4 triggers are refunds, suspected abuse, or a violation of the Dev Terms, the ToS or
   third-party store terms. Stars on a bot's balance are "owned by Telegram" (§6.2.2), and termination brings no
   compensation (§10). O5 stands, on the right clause.
5. **Telegram Ads rules cover the promoted product, not only the creative.** §5 applies "to the products they
   promote, whether implied or explicit". An ad with a clean creative for a bot that also carries an 18+ sexual track is
   still in scope. This turns O2 (same bot vs separate bot) into a promotion question as well as a flagging question.
6. **Selling 18+ content to minors is banned, even though verifying age is not required.** 436-FZ art. 16 part 6 bans
   selling information products prohibited for children to under-18s, and the seller may check an ID or MAX in case of
   doubt. Read with KoAP 6.17 part 1 (legal entity 20-50 k RUB, confiscation or suspension up to 90 days, ed.
   04.08.2026), an 18+ chapter sold to a 14-17 user is the administrative scenario even when the content is
   non-pornographic. INTERPRETATION: whether a Stars purchase is a "sale" in this sense.
7. **The MAX law did touch 18+ content (569-FZ amended 436-FZ art. 16 part 6).** It is a seller's right in a sale; no
   bot integration was found.
8. **ESIA access is not a bank/insurer list.** Under Decree 1382 p. 2, an organisation connects only where federal law
   or a President/Government act requires it, or the Government Commission approves. The conclusion that a game has no
   ESIA path is unchanged.
9. **436-FZ art. 5 part 2 is quoted from a stale edition.** The current text (ed. 29.12.2025) adds item 4.4,
   "refusal to have children" (411-FZ, 2024-11-23), and item 3.1, sexual violence, next to 4.1, non-traditional
   relations. These matter for romance plots, because they decide what is "18+" and what must never reach the 14-17
   track.
10. **Romance Club proves separate-app practice, not an erotica lane.** The source shows a separate app, an 18+ rating
    and same-sex routes removed. It says nothing on erotica in the RU build. Litnet, the other precedent, is a snippet
    only. So no verified Russian precedent shows 18+ *erotica* being tolerated in an app.

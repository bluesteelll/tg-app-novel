# Verify pass D (adversarial): reports 08 (markets and hedges) and 09 (access, audience, minors)

Date 2026-10-31. Method: the URL each claim cites was opened first, with WebFetch on 2026-10-31. Then 14 WebSearch
calls were run for the counter-evidence and the missing primaries. Legal readings are INTERPRETATION, not legal
advice. No logins and no downloads were used, and no owner identity went into any request.

Verdicts:
- **CONFIRMED**: the source says it.
- **CORRECTED**: the claim is partly wrong or its attribution is wrong; the right value and source are given.
- **UNSUPPORTED**: the cited source does not say it, or the source cannot be opened (the row says which).
- **STALE**: a newer source changes the claim.
- **MISLABELLED**: the evidence label is wrong.

Row numbering:
- `08:n` and `09:n` are the numbered "Key claims for verification" of each report.
- `B:n` rows are the brief's load-bearing claims that the numbered lists do not cover.

## Cross-report contradictions

1. **Fragment KYC.** 08 (TL;DR and §2) says "Fragment (TON/Gram, KYC)", sourced to the promotional dtf guide. 09 found no KYC. The primary pages (fragment.com/stars/buy and fragment.com/terms) mention no KYC, and the terms speak of anonymous purchases. 09 is right.
2. **Google Play as a Stars route.** 08 quotes a guide saying App Store and Google Play "work only by chance". 09 shows Play billing has been paused for Russia since 2022-03-10 (DOCUMENTED). Since 2022-08-02 Google also waives its own billing policy for RU payments. So in a Play build, Telegram may show its own checkout, but the Stars ToS §2 scopes @PremiumBot to the *direct* Android build. Neither report resolves which route a Play-build user in Russia actually sees. It is a live check.
3. **Cloudflare 16 KB.**
   - 08: "first ~16 KB", 2026 status unchecked (gap).
   - 09: "per connection", doc of 2026-04-23. 09 closes 08's gap.
   - The vendor itself is inconsistent: the blog says per asset, the doc says per connection.
   - 09's "splitting won't help" inference is unsupported (row 31).
4. **"WEB Proxy" in tdesktop.** The two reports are compatible. 08 says the source is silent on Mini Apps. 09 says it "concerns Telegram's own traffic, not Mini Apps". Kod Durova (2026-08-22) describes it as MTProxy over a WebView/HTTPS/WebSocket transport and says nothing about Mini Apps. 09's "not Mini Apps" is an inference, and PR #31166 (SOCKS5 only) is the actual Mini App path.
5. **Ruble price per Star.** 09 uses three different bases:
   - store 2.09 ₽ (DOCUMENTED, 2026-10-31);
   - @PremiumBot 1.98 ₽ (2024-25);
   - vc.ru 1.8-2.5 ₽.
   08 has no ruble price. The synthesis should use 2.09 ₽ as the only dated, documented figure and present the others as CLAIMED.
6. **MAX distribution.** 08: "removed from the App Store (sanctions) and then Google Play". iphones.ru (2026-06-03) still listed Google Play as an install route at the time. Verstka (2026-07-21) puts the Play removal on "last Thursday" (≈ 2026-07-16). The timeline is consistent; it is not a contradiction.
7. **VK developers.** 08 says foreign developers are not addressed (rules) and that payout depends on legal form and tax residency. The VK monetization overview (opened in this pass) requires the payout user to be ИП, самозанятый or юрлицо. That moves VK next to MAX: gated on a Russian business form (INTERPRETATION). Neither report states this outright.

## Most decision-relevant corrections

- **Stars from Russia: degraded, not closed.**
  - Carrier billing ended 2026-04-01 (press, consistent). Apple's payment-methods page (2026-08-07) lists no Russia entry.
  - Marked-up App Store gift cards remain (T-Ж; Mash via a snippet). "Last mass funding route" overstates it.
  - @PremiumBot: the only opened 2026 source (appleinsider, upd. 2026-10-07) says cards, SBP and SberPay work for Premium. 09's counter-source (dtf 5199168) does not say what it is cited for. The Stars ToS limits @PremiumBot to the direct Android build, Web and Desktop.
  - The synthesis should frame the issue as "RU Stars funding is uncertain; live-check @PremiumBot /stars on a Russian account", not "every route is closed".
- **Fragment is crypto-only with no KYC on primary pages.** Drop 08's "KYC".
- **The debit clause is Stars ToS §5**, not §3. It debits the *player's* balance. The revenue effect on a bot that was already paid is still a gap.
- **VPN 40 % comes from SecurityLab** (Russian Field, 2026-04-15..22, n = 1600), not from Carnegie. Age split: 18-29 61 %, 30-44 50 %.
- **Telegram Ads reseller floors** (€0.15 / €0.20):
  - They are reported 2026-08-28 for **EUR reseller accounts only**. The effective date is not stated. The user-targeting floor is unchanged.
  - Gram/TON self-serve pricing is unchanged (official floor 0.1 Toncoin/Gram).
  - The ≈ €0.49 average has no named source.
- **The Kazakhstan launch month is disputed** (Oct 2025 per Magnetto, Dec 2025 per AdIndex), with no Telegram announcement. CIS CPMs remain vendor self-publication (CLAIMED).
- **RKN letter 03-72707** is a tax/ERIR reply to MinFin. It does not say non-Russia-aimed ads fall outside 38-FZ, so it is not a legal safe harbour for Russian-language creatives (INTERPRETATION).
- **Network reach:**
  - Only Cloudflare's 16 KB cap is documented (doc 2026-04-23). Hetzner, DO and OVH have only "reportedly similar restrictions" (The Record).
  - tdesktop PR #31166 (open, ready for review 2026-09-23) would fix the Desktop WebView for **SOCKS5 only, not MTProto**.
  - The "sub-16 KB split won't help" reasoning is unsupported.
- **VK as a hedge is gated like MAX:**
  - VK payouts require ИП, самозанятый or юрлицо. Goods sales need CBR-licensed operators.
  - The 45 % figure is from 2021, "not final", and per press it is the withdrawal commission. No 2026 figure exists.
- **Smaller legal and ads fixes:**
  - Art. 175 plaintiffs include adoptive parents and the guardian.
  - Apple's "Ask to Buy can't be turned off under 18" is qualified by "depending on the child's state, country, or region".
  - VK Ads cl. 4.1's 18+ list also covers medicines.

## Most decision-relevant corrections

1. **Telega.io-style broadcast exchanges take our bot token** (row 23). The token is the initData HMAC key and the full Bot API credential. Handing it over breaks principles 0, 1 and 3 and exposes payments and refunds. This is a disqualifier separate from §5.2(b) and 38-FZ art. 18 (INTERPRETATION on DOCUMENTED mechanisms).
2. **The AdsGram SDK sends the signed initData field set and motion-sensor readings per ad request** (row 19: Ed25519 `signature` + `data_check_string`, plus `X-Accelerometer`/`X-Gyroscope`/`X-Device-Orientation` headers). The 152-FZ / Y16 transfer is therefore concrete, not hypothetical. `start_param` campaign and invite codes would reach AdsGram (observed in code, not in traffic).
3. **Developer Terms §7.4 reaches ad content inside a Mini App** (row 10). Non-TON crypto promotion "directly or indirectly", and links to such platforms, are banned. A crypto blocklist is a Terms requirement, and AdsGram documents only a Gambling/Adult opt-out.
4. **The VPN-advertising ban (38-FZ art. 5 p. 10.8, from 2025-09-01, broad FAS reading)** is missing from 13 (row 55). Clips, landing, bot copy and creator briefs must never say "use a VPN" or link to one.
5. **TikTok is not No. 1 among 12-17 by daily reach** (VK 63 % vs 61 %, 2024). It is No. 1 by time: "absolute leader", 25 % of teen online time, Mediascope CEO, 2026-06-03 (rows 31-33).
6. **"The YouTube block did not shrink it" is an inference** (row 37). The +4 % and +24 % figures have no stated baselines, and Jan-May includes pre-block January. Keep the figures and drop the causal wording.
7. **The `t.me` outage was a registry serverHold on 2026-07-13/14 (~1 day)**, reported by many outlets, not a single-source "DNS failure on ~07-16" (row 50). The own-domain landing ask is unchanged.
8. **The 73 / 48 min teen-time figures have no stated period** (row 30). 13 KC9 (Levada 39 %) and the KC11 specifics (Apr-15 restriction, 15 GB, 469) are unsupported at the cited URLs (rows 52, 54).
9. **The arithmetic is correct, but the ads-vs-Stars ranking is price-driven** (Arithmetic check). Below ~5.4 Stars per payer-day (e.g. the 1-Star placeholder), rewarded ads out-earn Stars in the base case. The synthesis should present this as dependent on Q3.
